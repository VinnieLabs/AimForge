🎯 AimForge

TEST YOUR AIM. FIND YOUR SENS. MASTER EVERY GAME.

AimForge is a modern aim-training and sensitivity platform built for competitive gamers. Test your flicks, tracking, reaction time, precision, and target switching, analyze your performance, discover a sensitivity range to experiment with, and convert your settings across supported games.

🧪 Aim Tests

AimForge measures different aspects of aiming, including:

Flicking — speed and accuracy when quickly moving between targets.

Tracking — how consistently you follow moving targets.

Precision — your ability to hit smaller targets and make accurate adjustments.

Reaction Time — how quickly you respond to new targets.

Target Switching — how efficiently you move between multiple targets.

Results can be compared over time to track improvement.

🧠 Sensitivity Analysis

AimForge uses your test performance to help you experiment with different sensitivity settings. Rather than claiming there is one universally perfect sensitivity, AimForge provides a recommended range based on your individual test results.
